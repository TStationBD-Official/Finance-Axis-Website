Delete Your Account & Data

You're always in control of your FinanceAxis data. Below are two ways to permanently delete your account.

Option 1: Delete In-App (Instant)

If you have the app installed and can sign in, this is the fastest way to delete your account and all of your data immediately:

  1. Open FinanceAxis and sign in to your account.
  2. Go to Settings → Profile.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning, check the confirmation box, and tap Delete Account again to confirm.
  5. If prompted, re-enter your password or sign in with Google again to confirm your identity.

Your account and data are deleted immediately once this process completes.

Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you no longer have the app installed, can't sign in, or prefer to request deletion directly, email us:

  • To: support.financeaxis@gmail.com
  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Include: the email address registered to your FinanceAxis account, so we can verify and locate it

We process email deletion requests within 30 days of receipt.

What Gets Deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes the following from our servers:

  • Your profile (name, email association, preferences)
  • All transactions, accounts, budgets, and categories
  • All account-to-account transfer history
  • Your subscription record

Important: Deleting your FinanceAxis account does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription. Google Play billing is managed separately by Google - to stop future charges, cancel your subscription directly from the Google Play Store app (Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) before or after deleting your account.

Guest / Local-Only Accounts

If you used FinanceAxis in Guest Mode, your data was never uploaded to our servers - it only exists on your device. Deleting the app, or using the in-app Delete Account option, removes it completely with nothing further needed on our end.

Questions about this process? See our Privacy Policy or contact support.financeaxis@gmail.com.
