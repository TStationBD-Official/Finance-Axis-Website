3. Accounts, Data Storage, and Synchronization

Cloud Synchronization

If you sign in using Google Sign-In or email authentication, your financial data may be securely synchronized using Firebase services to enable multi-device access and data recovery.

Google Drive Backup

Google Drive Backup is available exclusively for Pro users.

  • Backups are stored in the user's personal Google Drive account
