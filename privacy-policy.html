Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. FinanceAxis ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect financial data you manually input, including transactions, budget goals, and account balances. If you sign in, we collect your email address and profile name.

2. Third-Party Services

We use the following services to provide functionality:

  • Firebase: For cloud synchronization and authentication.
  • Google Drive API: To allow you to backup data to your own account. We only access files created by our App.
  • Yandex Mobile Ads: To display ads to free users.
  • Google Play Billing: To process Pro subscriptions.
  • bKash / Nagad: As an alternative Pro payment option for users in Bangladesh. These payments are manually verified by our team rather than processed through an automated gateway.

3. Data Ownership

Your financial data belongs to you. We do not sell or share your personal financial records with third parties.

4. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Settings → Profile → Delete Account), or by requesting deletion via email if you no longer have the app installed. See our Account Deletion page for full instructions on what gets deleted and how long it takes.

5. Contact

If you have questions, contact us at support.financeaxis@gmail.com.
